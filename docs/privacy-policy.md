# Privacy Policy — XDownloader

XDownloader processes only the data needed for the bot to work: the YouTube/Spotify links you send, your Telegram user ID (used to deliver the file), and the chat ID.

Downloaded files are stored temporarily on the server and deleted immediately after they are sent. Logs do not contain the contents of the links you share.

Downloads are performed through third-party services (yt-dlp/spotdl) and the YouTube/Spotify servers; their policies do not apply to your data.

The bot does not collect, sell, or share personal data with third parties, beyond the technically necessary minimum required to deliver files in Telegram.
