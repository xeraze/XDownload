# XDownloader

A Telegram bot that downloads **audio and video** for you — just send it a link.

## Supported platforms

- **YouTube** — audio and video
- **Spotify** — audio (320 kbit/s)
- **SoundCloud** — audio
- **TikTok**, **Instagram**, **Facebook**, **Reddit**, **Pinterest**, **Likee** — audio and video

## How to use

1. Open the bot in Telegram and press **Start**.
2. Send any link from the platforms above — in a private chat or in a group.
3. Choose **🎵 Audio (mp3)** or **🎬 Video (mp4)**.
4. For video you can pick the quality: **480 / 720 / 1080**.

Spotify and SoundCloud are audio-only, so the bot shows just the audio button for them.

The file is sent straight back to the chat — save it or share it however you like.

## Notes

- Made for personal use. Downloading from YouTube or Spotify may violate their terms of service, and Telegram can block public music bots.
